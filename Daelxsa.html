Why TrustRida

Proof before trust, not trust before proof.

Every online sale between strangers starts with the same standoff: someone has to go first. TrustRida changes what the buyer can see before that moment, so going first stops being a gamble.

Get started See how it works

The standoff

Both sides are protecting themselves from the same thing.

The buyer will not pay a stranger. The seller will not ship to one. So the sale dies in the chat, or one side takes a risk they should not have to take.

The honest seller loses the most. They are paying for the behaviour of everyone who scammed that buyer before them, and no amount of screenshots, testimonials or “trust me sis” fixes it, because all of that is exactly what a scammer sends too.

How it works today

  • Screenshots of past deliveries, easily edited
  • A phone number that may not be answered tomorrow
  • Reviews on the seller's own page, written by the seller's friends
  • A bank transfer receipt that proves money left, nothing more
  • “My rider is on the way” and then silence

With TrustRida

  • A live page, not an image, that the buyer opens themselves
  • Rider identity and registration checked before dispatch
  • The bike plate tied to this order, matchable at the door
  • Pickup and delivery timestamped as they happen
  • One record the seller, rider and buyer all read

Honest comparisons

“Why not just use what already exists?”

Fair question. Here is where each alternative stops, and where we start.

Delivery apps

They verify riders well — for their own platform. What they do not give an independent seller is a shareable link a buyer can check before deciding to pay. That gap is the whole reason TrustRida exists.

Bank transfer receipts

A receipt proves money moved. It says nothing about who is bringing the goods, whether they were ever collected, or whether anyone is actually on the way. It is proof of the wrong half of the deal.

Ratings and reviews

Reviews describe a seller's past, on a page the seller controls. They cannot tell you anything about the specific person who will knock on your door in forty minutes.

Escrow services

Escrow holds the money. It does not tell you whether the rider is real or whether the goods left the shop. TrustRida verifies the people and records the handover — the part escrow cannot see.

What we stand on

Four rules we will not trade away.

A trust product that overpromises is just another thing to be scammed by.

Say what was checked

We show which checks were done and which were not. No blanket badge that means nothing.

Tie it to the order

Verification belongs to a specific delivery, not to a profile that can be reused forever.

Work on any phone

No app to download, no account to open a link, and light enough for a weak connection.

Never promise an outcome

We do not guarantee delivery, refunds or behaviour. We give you better information to decide with.

What actually changes

The same delivery, with the guessing removed.

The buyer stops guessing

They see the rider's checked details and the plate number before any money moves, and can match both at the door.

The seller stops arguing

The record shows what was handed to which rider and when, so a disagreement starts from facts instead of memory.

The rider stops being a suspect

Verified once and expected on arrival, instead of being interrogated at every gate by someone who was never told they were coming.
