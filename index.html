How verification works

What gets verified

Specific to the delivery, not a general profile.

A badge on a profile page is easy to fake and easy to reuse. TrustRida ties each check to the order in front of you, so the buyer is looking at the rider actually on the way.

  • Identity information checked before dispatch
  • Government or company registration ID recorded
  • Bike plate number attached to this order
  • Phone contact verified and reachable
  • Dispatch clearance shown as live status

Verified does not mean risk-free

We tell you what has been checked and what has not. We never promise an outcome. Read our limits.
