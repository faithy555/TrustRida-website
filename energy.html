HOW IT WORKS

Verification should be specific, not a screenshot.

A verification page should connect the rider to the actual delivery instead of relying only on a WhatsApp photo, phone number, review or payment screenshot.

1

Identity

The rider's identity information is checked and displayed as part of the verification profile.

2

Registration

A government or company registration ID can be recorded as part of the rider's verification information.

3

Vehicle

The rider's bike licence plate number is connected to the verification profile.

4

Contact & status

The phone contact and current dispatch clearance status are shown so the buyer can check before payment.

HONEST VERIFICATION

Verified does not mean risk-free.

Trust depends on transparency. Trustrida should never claim that verification guarantees the behaviour, safety or delivery outcome of another person.

What verification tells you

  • Which rider information has been checked.
  • Which registration information is recorded.
  • The bike plate associated with the profile.
  • Whether the phone contact has been verified.
  • Whether the dispatch clearance is currently active.

What we do NOT guarantee

  • We do not guarantee that a delivery will be completed successfully.
  • Verification does not guarantee that goods cannot be damaged, lost or delayed.
  • Verification does not guarantee a rider's future behaviour.
  • Verification does not automatically guarantee a refund or replacement.
  • A verified profile should not replace your own judgement before making a payment.
SOMETHING WENT WRONG?

Don't disappear into a WhatsApp chat.

If the rider, vehicle, contact details or delivery do not match the verification information, stop and report the issue through the Trustrida reporting route.

Report a problem
01 — Stop the transaction Do not continue with payment if the verification details do not match.
02 — Record the mismatch Keep the verification page and relevant transaction information.
03 — Contact support Submit the problem through the reporting channel for review.
04 — Escalate when necessary If there is suspected fraud or immediate danger, contact the appropriate authorities.
ACCESSIBILITY CHECK

You should not need a mouse to use Trustrida.

This page is designed so that important information and actions can be reached with a keyboard alone.

✓ Keyboard navigation

Interactive links can be reached using Tab, activated with Enter, and reversed with Shift + Tab.

✓ Skip navigation

Keyboard users can press Tab when the page loads and select "Skip to main content."

✓ Visible focus

Focused elements receive a visible outline so keyboard users can identify where they are.

✓ Logical structure

The page uses headings, navigation, sections, articles and labelled regions to make its structure easier to understand.

✓ Reduced motion

Users who prefer reduced motion are not forced into smooth scrolling effects.

✓ Responsive layout

Content adapts to smaller screens without hiding essential verification information.
