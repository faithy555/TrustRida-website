How it works

Verification should be specific, not a screenshot.

A verification page should connect a rider to the actual delivery, instead of relying on a WhatsApp photo, a phone number, a review or a payment screenshot — all of which describe the past, or nothing at all.

1

Identity

The rider's identity information is checked and shown as part of the verification profile.

2

Registration

A government or company registration ID is recorded against the rider's profile.

3

Vehicle

The bike licence plate is connected to the profile, so it can be matched at your door.

4

Contact & status

The phone contact and current dispatch clearance are shown so a buyer can check before paying.

Honest verification

Verified does not mean risk-free.

Trust depends on transparency. TrustRida will never claim that verification guarantees the behaviour, safety or delivery outcome of another person.

What verification tells you

  • Which rider information has been checked
  • Which registration information is recorded
  • The bike plate associated with the profile
  • Whether the phone contact has been verified
  • Whether dispatch clearance is currently active

What we do not guarantee

  • That a delivery will be completed successfully
  • That goods cannot be damaged, lost or delayed
  • A rider's future behaviour
  • An automatic refund or replacement
  • That you can skip your own judgement before paying

Something went wrong?

Do not disappear into a WhatsApp chat.

If the rider, vehicle, contact details or delivery do not match the verification information, stop and report it. A mismatch is worth pausing over, even when the person in front of you sounds convincing.

Report a problem Contact support
01 — Stop the transaction Do not continue with payment if the verification details do not match what you can see.
02 — Record the mismatch Keep the verification link and any relevant transaction information.
03 — Contact support Submit the problem through the reporting channel so it can be reviewed.
04 — Escalate when necessary If there is suspected fraud or immediate danger, contact the appropriate authorities.

Accessibility

You should not need a mouse to use TrustRida.

Verification is useless if the person who needs it cannot reach it. Every page is built so the important information and actions work with a keyboard alone.

Keyboard navigation

Links and controls are reachable with Tab, activated with Enter, and reversed with Shift + Tab.

Skip navigation

Press Tab when a page loads and the first stop is “Skip to main content”.

Visible focus

Focused elements get a high-contrast outline, so keyboard users always know where they are.

Logical structure

Headings, landmarks and labelled regions make each page understandable to a screen reader.

Reduced motion

If your device asks for reduced motion, transitions and smooth scrolling are switched off.

Responsive layout

Content adapts to small screens without hiding any of the verification information.
