How it works

One order, one record, three people watching it.

The whole flow takes minutes to set up and nothing to install. Here is exactly what happens, and what each person sees at each step.

Start an order See a sample record

The flow

From “can I trust you” to “received, thanks”.

Five steps. Each one leaves a mark on the record that all three sides can see.

  1. The seller opens the order

    Item, buyer's area, and which rider is carrying it. TrustRida creates one page with a reference code. Nothing is public — only whoever holds the link can open it.

  2. The rider is verified and attached

    Identity, registration ID and bike plate are checked and recorded against this order. A rider verified last month still shows their clearance status for today.

  3. The buyer checks before paying

    The seller sends the link. The buyer opens it on any phone, with no account, and sees who is coming and what has been confirmed. Then they decide.

  4. Pickup is stamped on the record

    When the rider collects the item, that moment is written to the record with a timestamp. The buyer's page updates — nobody has to send a “he has left” message.

  5. The buyer confirms delivery

    On arrival the buyer matches the face and plate against the page, takes the item, and confirms. The record closes with both sides agreeing on what happened.

When it goes wrong

A mismatch is a stop sign, not a small detail.

The record is useful precisely because it can disagree with what someone is telling you. If the page and the person at your gate do not match, that is the system working.

Do not continue on a mismatch

Stop the payment, keep the link, and report it. Full steps are on the verification page.

Different plate number

The bike outside does not carry the plate on the record. Do not hand over money or goods.

Different person

A rider sent a friend. Ask the seller to reassign the order so the record matches reality.

The link will not open

A real TrustRida page always loads live. A screenshot instead of a link is a warning.

Nothing arrived

The record shows whether pickup ever happened, which is where the conversation should start.

Try it on your next order.

Set up one handover and send the link. If your buyer does not find it easier than screenshots, nothing is lost.
