How payment and handover work

The same four steps on every order, every round.

  1. You order and join the round

    Confirm the item and your delivery area with the seller. Your order is added to the next collection round and gets its own reference.

  2. You check the rider before paying

    The record shows who is collecting, what has been verified about them, and the bike plate for that round. Nothing is hidden behind a payment.

  3. Pickup is logged as it happens

    When Alex collects your order, that moment is written to the round record with a timestamp — not typed in afterwards by the seller.

  4. You confirm delivery

    Match the rider and plate at your door, take the item, and confirm on the record. Both sides now agree, in writing, on what was delivered and when.

“Why should I go first, if I have been burned before?”

Honestly: you are still choosing to pay a person you have not met. What changes is how much you know when you make that choice. The round record above is not something the seller typed up — each line is written the moment the rider collects an order, which is why one of them still reads pending pickup instead of quietly showing everything as done.

Anyone can check it before deciding, not only after a problem shows up. That is the whole of what we are offering, and we would rather say it plainly than dress it up as a guarantee.

What this record does not do

It does not guarantee the goods, hold your money, or promise a refund. See the full limits before you rely on it.
