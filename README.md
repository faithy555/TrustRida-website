TrustRida
TrustRida is a platform designed to build transparency, reliability, and mutual trust between vehicle owners and riders/drivers. It provides a structured framework for managing agreements, performance tracking, and clear accountability.
Key Features
 * Accountability Tracking: Transparent logging of vehicle usage, trips, and operational metrics.
 * Agreement Management: Clear, accessible contracts and terms between owners and drivers.
 * Trust & Performance Scores: Data-backed ratings to foster long-term reliability and fair partnerships.
 * Dispute Reduction: Centralized records to ensure both parties operate with mutual confidence.
