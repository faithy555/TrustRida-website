Questions before you pay.

Trust & identity

TrustRida shows a photo, a government or company ID reference, and the bike plate number tied to that specific delivery — not a general profile. Match the face and the plate before you open your door or hand over money.

IDs are checked against the rider’s registered details before a link goes live, so a mismatched or fabricated ID does not generate a valid page. If a page will not load properly, treat that as the answer.

Every genuine link opens live on the TrustRida site and shows current rider status. A screenshot cannot update, and it cannot be opened as a page. If someone sends you an image instead of a working link, do not proceed.

Sellers can assign a different rider per order, so always open the link for that specific delivery. Do not assume it is the same rider as last time, even from a seller you have used before.

We have to be straight with you here: TrustRida confirms that a rider is real and dispatched, but it is not a payment escrow and does not hold your money. Agree payment terms — part payment, pay on arrival — directly with the seller.

A transfer receipt only proves that money left your account. It says nothing about who is bringing your goods, or whether anything was ever collected. TrustRida verifies the person and records the handover, which is the half a receipt cannot cover.

Why this over alternatives

Those apps verify riders well — for their own platform and their own orders. What they do not give an independent seller is a shareable link a buyer can check before deciding to pay. TrustRida is built for exactly that gap.

You are not really going first — you are verifying the rider before payment, which reverses the order the scam depends on. We will not tell you the risk is zero, because it is not. We will tell you what has been checked, and what has not.

It would be, if it were a badge on a profile. Ours is attached to a specific order, generated at the time, and only reachable through a live page. That is a much harder thing to counterfeit than an image.
