How payment and handover work

The same steps for every order, every round.

  1. You order and confirm the round. [How the buyer actually confirms — DM, form, etc.]
  2. Payment holds with Trustrida, not the seller. You pay through the platform. The seller doesn't receive it yet.
  3. [Rider name] scans your order at pickup. That's the moment it's added to the round's record, with a timestamp you can check.
  4. You confirm delivery, then the seller is paid out. One tap on your side releases the payment. If it doesn't arrive, the payment simply reverts to you.

Why go first, if I've been burned before

You're not paying the seller first — you're paying Trustrida, and Trustrida only releases the money once you've confirmed the order reached you. The round record above isn't something the seller wrote; it's logged the moment [rider name] collects each order, so anyone can check it before deciding, not just after a problem shows up.
